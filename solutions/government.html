The Challenge

Public Services Need Digital Transformation

Public service facilities often need to connect document handling, identity checks, queue guidance and information access with existing local software workflows.

⏱️

Long Queues & Limited Hours

Citizens wait hours for simple services. Service windows close at 5 PM, leaving working people underserved.

Lack of Transparency

Manual processes make it hard to track application status, creating public distrust.
