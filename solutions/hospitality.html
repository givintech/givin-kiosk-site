Hotel Self Check-In Solutions

Automate guest arrival with self-service check-in kiosks — ID verification, digital registration and key card issuance in under 60 seconds.

The Challenge

Guests Want Speed — Hotels Need Efficiency

The hotel industry faces a dual challenge: rising guest expectations for instant, frictionless experiences and the constant pressure to reduce operational costs. Front desk queues during peak hours damage first impressions and strain limited staff.

🏨

Peak Hour Bottlenecks

Check-in queues during afternoon peak hours can exceed 20 minutes — a terrible first impression.

💰

Labor Costs Rising

24/7 front desk staffing is expensive. Hotels need to do more with fewer people.

🌙

Late Night Arrivals

Guests arriving after midnight often face unmanned desks or slow service.

Our Solution

60-Second Self Check-In

From passport scan to room key — the entire check-in process automated in under a minute.

✅

Passport & ID Scanning

Facial Recognition Verification

Key Card Issuance
