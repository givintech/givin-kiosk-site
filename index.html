Manufacturing Capability

From Sheet Metal to Tested Terminal

Our manufacturing network supports sheet-metal fabrication, enclosure finishing, wiring, component assembly, final integration and inspection for project-based kiosk production.

Project Production Flow

A practical OEM/ODM path

  1. Requirement reviewApplication, screen, software environment, modules and installation method.
  2. Mechanical proposalEnclosure layout, access panels, paper path, mounting and serviceability.
  3. Prototype confirmationAppearance, interfaces, branding and module operation reviewed before volume production.
  4. Fabrication and assemblySheet-metal processing, finishing, wiring, subassembly and final integration.
  5. Inspection and packingProject-defined electrical, functional, appearance and packaging checkpoints.

Capacity, lead time and compliance documentation are confirmed against the final BOM, order quantity and destination market.
